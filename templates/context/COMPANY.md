# Company

Who we are and what we can sustain. Plain facts, not aspirations.

This file is what stops the pipeline building bulletproof scalable infrastructure for a product with forty users and one person maintaining it. It is the justification behind the minimum-viable rule — the rule lives in the skills, the calibration lives here.

> **Living file.** When the numbers change the constraint loosens on its own, so a stale COMPANY.md silently lies and every run calibrates against a company that no longer exists. Record the last review date below and revisit it on a set cadence.

**Last reviewed:** `<date>`

## Team `[ask]`

Headcount. Who works on product, and how much of their time actually reaches it. Name the constraint honestly — "one person, evenings" produces very different specs from "four engineers".

## Stage `[ask]`

Pre-launch, early users, growing, established. What the next milestone is.

## Users and traffic `[ask]`

Current user count, active users, traffic. Real numbers, including when they're small — small numbers are the most load-bearing facts in this file.

## Resources `[ask]`

Budget for services and infrastructure. What the company can afford to run and to maintain, which are different questions.

## Infrastructure `[derive → confirm]`

What is running today: hosting, database, services, monitoring. Derived from the codebase and config where possible, confirmed by a human.

## Constraints `[ask]`

Standing limits any feature has to respect: compliance, contractual commitments, platform dependencies, things that cannot go down.
