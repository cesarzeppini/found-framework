# 12 Trust and safety (T)

For products where users post things others see. Apple (guideline 1.2) and Google Play require the first four for apps with user content; the EU Digital Services Act asks similar things of online platforms.

- **Objectionable content is filtered before it is posted,** at least for the obvious cases. `[UGC]`
- **Anyone can report a post, message, profile or image,** and reports reach a person who answers in a set time. `[UGC]` Prove: report something and follow where it lands.
- **Users can block another user,** and a blocked user cannot reach them. `[UGC]` Prove: message a user who blocked you.
- **Contact details are published** so users can reach the company. `[UGC]`
- **Non-consensual intimate images, including AI-generated ones,** are prohibited, actively prevented and removed. Apple's guidelines now name them explicitly. `[UGC]`
- **Moderators can act:** hide content, suspend accounts, and see what was reported and decided. Actions are logged. `[UGC]`
- **Illegal content** has a reporting path and a decision process, per the Digital Services Act where it applies. `[UGC]`
