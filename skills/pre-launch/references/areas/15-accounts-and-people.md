# 15 Accounts and people (K)

Usually `read`, from a person, and dashboard.

- **Two-factor on every service with access:** code host, database, payments, hosting, monitoring, email provider, DNS, domain registrar, package registry. `[all]` Dashboard.
- **More than one owner** on each critical account, ideally company accounts, not personal ones. `[all]` Dashboard.
- **A list of who has access**, with former collaborators and old access tokens removed. `[all]` Dashboard.
- **The scope of every key in use** is the least it needs. `[all]`
- **Branch protection on main:** pull requests and passing checks required. `[all]` Dashboard.
- **Domain locked at the registrar, auto-renew on.** `[web]` Dashboard.
