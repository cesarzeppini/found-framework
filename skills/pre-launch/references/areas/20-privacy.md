# 20 Privacy (P)

**Reuses** compliance's "what to look for": personal data, processors, cookies, retention and location, read across the whole product instead of a diff.

- **A data map:** what personal data, where it is stored, why, for how long, which services receive it. `[all]`
- **Signed-out visitors see only what is meant to be public.** Public profiles show the minimum. `[all]` Prove: fetch profiles and the user directory signed out.
- **Exact locations, emails and phone numbers are not public** unless the product needs them to be. `[all]`
- **Deletion is defined:** what is removed, what is kept for the other party and why. `[all]`
- **Users can get a copy of their data.** Manual is fine at first. `[all]`
- **Data is stored where the privacy policy says,** and transfers outside the EU are covered. `[all]`
- **Logs and analytics collect only what is needed.** Third-party scripts are listed, including every outside host each visitor's browser contacts. `[all]`
