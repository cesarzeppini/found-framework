# LinkedIn

**Read:** `context/BRAND.md` → LinkedIn section, for register. `context/CHANNELS.md` → LinkedIn mechanics: character limit, where links go (body or first comment), image specs, hashtag convention. `SPEC.md` and the diff.

**Write:** `work/<feature>/out/linkedin.md`

## Shape

Hook, substance, close. The first line has to survive being the only line shown before "see more" — it carries the whole post's weight.

If CHANNELS.md puts links in the first comment, write that comment as a labelled second block in the same file. Never leave the link only in the body when the mechanics say otherwise.

## Rules

- Register comes from BRAND.md's LinkedIn section, not from what LinkedIn posts generally sound like. The platform's default voice is the thing a brand section exists to override.
- No engagement bait: no "thoughts?", no one-word-per-line ladders, no manufactured lesson.
- Claims bounded by the spec's success metric.
- Plain text. Social posts never become branded artifacts.

## Hand back

**Decisions, not activity** (RULES.md) — a few lines at most. The post is the deliverable.

- claims cut against the success metric
- where CHANNELS.md was silent on mechanics you had to decide — link placement, hashtags, the fold
- anything BRAND.md's LinkedIn section didn't cover, and what you assumed
